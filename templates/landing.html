How the Engine Works

Three-stage cognitive interpretation pipeline from English speech to Apple glyphs.

01

Syntactic Decomposition

The user's sentence is validated and analyzed as a whole thought, identifying semantic entities, verbal actions, spatial relationships, and temporal markers.

02

Multi-Tier Semantic Matching (LUT + Cloud/Ollama LLM)

Exact idioms hit the O(1) Look-Up Table (<0.05ms). Complex multi-clause sentences invoke the Semantic Sequence Planner to decode nuances like school (🏫) vs graduation (🎓).

03

Font Pack Validation & Real-Time WebSocket Delivery

Codepoints are validated against the 1,485 supported Apple iOS glyphs and broadcast over Socket.IO to room participants instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Navarasam.

How is Navarasam different from standard emoji keyboards? expand_more

Standard emoji keyboards only suggest isolated word replacements. Navarasam analyzes your full sentence, extracts relationships like spatial containment ("in the bag" → 📥 👜), negation ("don't like" → 🚫), and event sequence, translating the complete thought into a coherent narrative sequence.

How does 2-person real-time chatting work? expand_more

Click "Start Instant 2-Person Chat" or "Share" inside the app. It creates an isolated room over WebSocket and copies a direct Wi-Fi/LAN link. Send the link to a friend on your network or open it in a second window to chat in pure emojis with sub-millisecond sync!

Are emojis guaranteed to render identically on Android and Windows? expand_more

Yes! Navarasam embeds a validated Apple iOS emoji font (ios_emoji.ttf) containing 1,485 native glyphs. Every device sees identical, crisp Apple emoji glyphs regardless of browser or OS.
